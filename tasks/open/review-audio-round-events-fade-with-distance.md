# A fish secured, a cat captured, a rescue and a dig-out are placed in the world, so far players miss them

`play` places `secured`, `captured`, `rescue` and `dugOut` at the event's position through the inverse-distance panner (REF 2 m), like an impact. A dog in the house hears a fish secured at the hideout 25 m away at 2/25 of full level (−22 dB), under the music; a cat's teammates across the property hear its capture at −20 dB or less. GAME.md, Audio Direction: priority on gameplay-relevant feedback. These four change the round table and every player's plan the way the phase stingers do, and those are heard everywhere at full level (card 55).

## DoD
- The four round events are heard everywhere at full level (no panner), as the phase stingers are; a placed copy may stay for direction, at most at the same level.

## Acceptance
- With `?audio` on a client 25 m from the hideout, the `secured` fanfare's peak is within 1 dB of its peak at 2 m; before: 22 dB lower.

## Test
- None: the readout is the measurement.
