# Cats 1–3 in their final look, with their emotes: Проффесор, Золотий Батон, Страус з Межигір'я

GAME.md, Art Direction: per-character concept, then the skeleton, then detail by code; stylized low-poly, flat shading, one signature detail each. The concept lines are this card's; the numbers are the roster's (card 100); the rig is card 110's. Emotes: one to four per character, played from the event.

## DoD
- Проффесор: a stooped grey tabby, cream belly, a black mortarboard with a gold tassel; the tassel swings with the head. Emotes: the lecture (a paw raised, the tassel swings), the cap adjusted, a doze.
- Золотий Батон: a loaf-shaped golden cat, crust scores across its back, the head sunk into the body, a stub tail. Emotes: rising like dough, a roll onto its back, a glisten (the crust catches the light).
- Страус з Межигір'я: a black plumed body on long thin pink legs, a long cream neck, a plume fan for a tail. Emotes: the head buried (into the floor), a high-step strut, the plumes fanned.
- Each on the rig with every colour from its roster slots; a decal or a pattern where the concept asks (the crust, the plumes); at most 200 lines per file (ADR 0011's sign).
